# Viewing Submissions

## What Is the Submissions Page?

Every time someone fills out one of your forms, Endatix saves their answers as a **submission**. A submission is saved as soon as the respondent's first answers are stored, not only when they complete the form, so you also see forms that were started but not finished.

The **Submissions** page lists all submissions of one form in a grid. Use it to check how many responses have come in, follow up on incomplete ones, keep track of which submissions have been reviewed, and open a submission to read or edit its answers. Filters, sorting, and your choice of columns narrow a long list down to exactly what you need — for example, last week's completed submissions that nobody has reviewed yet.

## Open a Form's Submissions

Each form has its own Submissions page.

### From the Forms List

1. Click **Forms** in the Hub sidebar.
2. Find the form. Forms that aren't in a folder are listed under **Unassigned**. For a form in a folder, click the folder under **Folders**, or pick the folder in the list at the top of the page.
3. Hover over the form's card. **Design**, **Share Link**, and **Submissions** appear at the bottom of the card.
4. Click **Submissions**.

<ThemedImage
  alt="Form card for Appointment Scheduling showing 15 submissions, with Design, Share Link, and Submissions links at the bottom. An arrow points at Submissions."
  sources={{ light: formCardLight, dark: formCardDark }}
/>

The card also shows how many submissions the form has. While a form shows **No submissions yet**, its **Submissions** link is dimmed; open the page from the form's details instead.

### From the Form Details

Click a form's card anywhere except the links at the bottom. The form's details open in a panel on the right. Click **Submissions** at the top of the panel.

<ThemedImage
  alt="Form details panel for Appointment Scheduling with the Design, Share, and Submissions buttons above the Form details section. An arrow points at the Submissions button."
  sources={{ light: formPanelLight, dark: formPanelDark }}
/>

The same **Submissions** button is at the top of the form's **Form Overview** page, which opens when you leave the Form Builder with its back arrow.

### Return From a Submission

Click a row in the grid to open that submission's **Submission Details** page. **Back to submissions** takes you back to the list with the filters, sorting, and page you left it on.

The filters, sorting, page, and rows per page are also part of the page's web address. Bookmark a filtered list to come back to it later, or send the link to a colleague who has access to the form.

## Filter Submissions

Filters show only the submissions that match. Combine as many as you need: a submission is listed only when it matches every filter. The list goes back to its first page whenever you change a filter.

### Filter by Complete, Status, and Submission Type

Click a filter above the grid and select one or more options. When you select several options of the same filter, submissions that match any of them are listed — **New** and **Read** under **Status** lists the submissions that haven't been approved yet.

The filter button shows your selection — or, with more than two options selected or on a smaller screen, how many options are selected. To clear one filter, open it and click **Clear filters** at the bottom of its list.

### Filter by Date

**Last modified**, **Started**, **Completed**, and **Created** each have their own date filter:

1. Hover over the column header and click its menu icon.
2. Pick a **From** date, a **To** date, or both. The range includes both days.
3. Click **Apply**.

<ThemedImage
  alt="Completed column menu with Asc, Desc, From and To date fields, Apply and Clear buttons, and Hide. An arrow points at the menu icon in the Completed column header."
  sources={{ light: columnMenuLight, dark: columnMenuDark }}
/>

To remove the date filter, open the menu again and click **Clear**. Date filters on different columns combine — for example, submissions created in March and completed in April.

### Filter by Submitter

The **Submitter** column menu — and the **Email** column menu, when your workspace shows submitter emails — has a **Filter** box instead of dates. Type the respondent's full ID or email address and click **Apply**. The filter looks for an exact match, so part of an ID or address doesn't find anything.

### Recognize Active Filters

<ThemedImage
  alt="Submissions filtered to Complete: Yes and sorted by Completed, newest first. The Completed column header shows a down arrow and a highlighted filter icon, which an arrow points at, and a Reset menu appears next to the filters."
  sources={{ light: sortedFilteredLight, dark: sortedFilteredDark }}
/>

* The **Complete**, **Status**, and **Submission Type** buttons show the options you selected.
* A column with an active date or submitter filter shows a highlighted filter icon in its header, even when you aren't hovering over it.
* A **Reset Filters** button — or a **Reset** menu when the list is also sorted — appears next to the filters.

When no submission matches your filters, the grid shows **No submissions match current filters.** with a **Reset Filters** button.

## Sort Submissions

Click a column title to sort by that column. Each click moves to the next step: ascending (arrow up), descending (arrow down), and back to the default order, newest first. You can also open a date or **Submitter** column's menu and click **Asc** or **Desc**, or **Clear sorting** to go back to the default order.

The list is sorted by one column at a time; sorting by another column replaces the previous sort. On the **Complete** column, hover over the check mark icon to show its sort button. Question and submitter detail columns can't be sorted.

:::info[Some columns sort only the current page]
**Last modified**, **Started**, **Completed**, and **Created** sort all of the form's submissions. **Complete**, **Submitter**, **Time**, and **Status** reorder only the submissions on the page you're looking at — page 2 is sorted on its own. To sort by one of these columns across more submissions, show more rows per page first.
:::

## Choose Columns

### Show and Hide Columns

Click **View** to open **Toggle Columns**, the list of every column the grid can show. A check mark means the column is shown; click a column to show or hide it. The menu closes after each click, so click **View** again to change another column.

<ThemedImage
  alt="Toggle Columns menu open under the View button. Complete, Last modified, Started, Completed, Created, Submitter, Time, Status, Full Name, and Appointment Type are checked; the other question columns, from Date Of Birth to Insurance Id, are not. A Reset Column Visibility button is next to View."
  sources={{ light: viewColumnsLight, dark: viewColumnsDark }}
/>

Below the standard columns, the list has a column for each of the form's questions, named after the question's name — a question named `appointmentType` becomes **Appointment Type**. Question columns are hidden until you turn them on and are added at the right end of the grid; scroll the grid sideways if they're out of view. They're available for these question types: Single-Line Input, Long Text, Checkboxes, Radio Button Group, Dropdown, Multi-Select Dropdown, Yes/No (Boolean), Rating Scale, Slider, and Ranking. Open a submission to see answers to other questions, such as matrices, dynamic panels, file uploads, and signatures.

To hide a date or **Submitter** column quickly, open its column menu and click **Hide**.

### Reorder Columns

Drag a column header to the left or right to move the column. The **⋯** column always stays first.

## Pages

The bottom of the grid shows how many submissions match — for example, **0 of 15 row(s) selected.** means 15 submissions — and which page you're on.

* **Rows per page** sets how many submissions a page shows: 10 (default), 20, 30, 40, or 50.
* The arrow buttons go to the previous and next page. On wide screens, the double arrows go to the first and last page.

## Work With a Submission

### Open a Submission

Click anywhere in a row to open the submission's **Submission Details** page, where you can read all of its answers.

### Change the Status

The **Status** column tracks your review progress. Click a submission's status and pick **New**, **Read**, or **Approved**. The change is saved immediately. Endatix doesn't change the status on its own — opening a submission leaves it **New** until you change it.

## Export Submissions

**Export Submissions** downloads the form's submissions as a file — such as a CSV or Excel file — to analyze them in a spreadsheet or another tool. Your workspace's settings decide which export formats are available.

## Important Considerations

* **Column choices stay in one browser.** Shown, hidden, and reordered columns are saved in the browser you use, not in your Endatix account. They don't carry over to another browser or device, aren't shared with colleagues, and are lost if you clear your browser's data.
* **Filters and sorting live in the web address.** They're kept when you go back from a submission or bookmark the page, but opening the Submissions page from the Forms list starts without them.
* **Date filters use whole days in UTC.** Dates in the grid are shown in your own time zone, but **From** and **To** count full days in Coordinated Universal Time. If you're far from UTC, a submission made late in the evening or early in the morning can fall on the neighboring day.
* **Some columns sort only the current page.** See [Sort Submissions](#sort-submissions).
* **Smaller screens show fewer columns.** On a narrow screen, **Created**, **Completed**, and **Time** are hidden until you turn them on in **View** — unless you've already changed the columns for that form.
* **Question columns follow the question's name, not its title.** Renaming a question in the Form Builder changes its column header. Give questions meaningful names, such as `appointmentType` rather than `question7`, so their columns are easy to recognize.
